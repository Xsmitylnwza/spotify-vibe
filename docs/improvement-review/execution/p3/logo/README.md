# P3-L1 — Vibe Studio logo concept directions

Concepts only; nothing under `electron/`, `scripts/` or the Studio UI was touched. Each direction has a master SVG (`dN-*.svg`, 64×64 viewBox, rounded-square tile) and a single-colour tray SVG (`dN-*-tray.svg`, `currentColor` on transparent; the sheet tints it dark on the light strip and white on the dark strip). Open `concept-sheet.html` (self-contained, no network/fonts); screenshots are `concept-sheet.png` (current + D1), `concept-sheet-2.png` (D2 + D3), `concept-sheet-3.png` (end of page: D3 + D4), taken from the sheet in an Orca browser tab. Palettes avoid Discord blurple and Spotify green; there is no GIPHY colour. Final PNG/ICO export would still need `build-icons.mjs` (pure-Node rasteriser) to be taught the new shapes — not done here.

**D1 · Soft Ghost (`d1-soft-ghost*.svg`)** — Idea: keep the ghost the owner knows, remove the bolt and square eye, use one round eye plus one wink, a gently scalloped hem, faint blush, cream body on a deep plum tile. Less dated: the current mark is busy and sits on the stock blurple gradient; this is simpler, warmer and rounder, with modern rounded geometry. Risks: closest to "more of the same" (the owner may still read it as old ghost); a generic ghost is a crowded space (Snapchat, many games); blush and gradient vanish below 32 px; the tray variant relies on the eye cut-outs, which are tiny at 16 px.

**D2 · Switch (`d2-switch*.svg`)** — Idea: two overlapping rounded "scene cards"; the amber filled one with a dot is the Live Scene, the outlined one is the previous/other scene — scene-switching and status in pure geometry. Less dated: no mascot, flat tool-like symbol in the style of current utility apps. Risks: loses the ghost character entirely (a deliberate break); an abstract symbol can read as "copy/duplicate windows"; the 16 px tray variant has a mask gap plus a hole that gets fine; orange + dark could resemble some dev tools.

**D3 · Nori mascot (`d3-nori*.svg`)** — Idea: a round headphone-wearing creature with eyes and a smile; gives Scenes a face that can later appear in empty states and Scene artwork. Less dated: full-colour sunset tile and a friendly character instead of a monochrome glyph. Risks: more personality means more maintenance (needs poses/expressions to pay off); headphones hint at music, which can mislead because the app is about any app; the tray version at 16 px loses the smile and reads as a headphone blob; I have not checked it against the actual Scene art style, so the match is inferred.

**D4 · V Monogram (`d4-v-monogram*.svg`)** — Idea: a heavy "V" with a presence dot floating where the right arm ends ("live"), deep petrol tile with cream and amber; intended to pair with a lowercase wordmark (the sheet shows the standard "Vibe Studio" text only — the lockup is not drawn). Less dated: bold monogram, a colour outside the Discord/Spotify space, strongest at 16 px. Risks: a lone V is generic and resembles several brands (Vimeo/Vercel-like Vs, check trademarks before adopting); no character left; teal can sit near Spotify green if pushed brighter; the dot must stay clear of the V in the tray version.
